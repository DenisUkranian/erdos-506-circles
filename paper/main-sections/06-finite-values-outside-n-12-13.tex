\section{Finite values outside $n=12,13$}

The verification bundle contains separate complete or review-ready proof packages for $n=8,9,10,11,14,15,16$.  Their role in this manuscript is summarized in \cref{tab:finite}.  Each package has its own proof note, source code, exact inputs, manifest and replay command.

\centering

\label{tab:finite}
\begin{tabular}{ccl}

\midrule
8  & 17 & exact construction; leave enumeration; Fano/parity obstructions \\
9  & 25 & exact block DFS; exceptional metric packet obstruction \\
10 & 33 & carrier collapse; exact $5+5$ and $6+4$ classifications \\
11 & 41 & complete profile chain; projective and radical-center terminals \\
14 & 73 & incidence reductions; pseudoline searches; exact profile terminals \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The finite packages use published line-arrangement inputs only in their stated forms.  Notably, eleven projective lines cannot have seventeen triple points \cite{dumnicki2016}, and the small-line classification work of K\"uhne, Szemberg and Tutaj-Gasi\'nska supplies additional extremal exclusions \cite{kuhne2024}.  The archive records an external-input ledger and avoids silently strengthening any cited theorem.
