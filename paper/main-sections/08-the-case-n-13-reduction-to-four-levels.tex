\section{The case $n=13$: reduction to four levels}

Assume for contradiction that $q\le60$.  The inherited finite proof gives a maximum generalized-circle block size at most six and excludes every profile with $B\le79$.  Hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Bge80}
 B\ge80.
\end{equation}

The $B=83$ package independently proves that no admissible 13-point line structure has $L\ge24$.  Thus $L\le23$, and the contradiction assumption gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B83bound}
 B=q+L\le83.
\end{equation}
It remains to eliminate the four levels $B=80,81,82,83$.
