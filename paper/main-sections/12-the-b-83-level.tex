\section{The $B=83$ level}

Pair partition and Melchior first show that $L\ge24$ leaves only the two line types $24T+6D$ and $25T+3D$, where $D$ denotes an ordinary double intersection in the dual arrangement.  Fresh rooted generalized-allowable-sequence searches exclude all cases, proving $L\le23$.

At $B=83$ under $q\le60$, one has $q=60$ and $L=23$.  Only two line types remain:
\[
 22T+Q+6D,
 \qquad
 23T+9D.
\]
All 44 rooted cases of the first type are excluded exactly.

For $23T+9D$, a line of triple-degree six exists.  Sending it to infinity reduces the finite allowable sequence to seventeen triple and nine double events.  Exhaustive enumeration visits 15,565,063 nodes and produces 96 labelled systems.  Explicit point permutations prove that all 96 represent one unlabelled 13-point triple system.

It remains to rule out its real projective realizability.  Choose a projective frame
\[
 P_0=(1,0,0),\quad P_1=(0,1,0),\quad P_2=(0,0,1),\quad P_3=(1,1,1),
\]
and use the forced incidences to normalize
\[
 P_{12}=(1,1,0),\quad P_6=(1,0,u),\quad P_7=(v,1,1),
 \quad P_9=(1,s,1),\quad P_{10}=(0,1,w).
\]
Distinctness gives $w\ne0$.  The remaining incidences imply polynomials
\[
 A=u^3+u^2w+uw-u-w=0,
\]
\[
 B=suv+sw-1=0,
 \quad E=sw+u^2+uw-u-w=0,
 \quad F=uv-u-1=0.
\]
From $B,F$ one gets $s(u+w+1)=1$; multiplying $E$ by $u+w+1$ gives
\[
 R=u^3+2u^2w+uw^2-uw-u-w^2=0.
\]
With $d=u^2+u-1$, exact expansion yields
\[
 d^2R-u(u-1)^3(u+1)
 =A\,(u^4+u^3w+2u^3-2u^2-2uw+w).
\]
Thus $A=R=0$ forces $u\in\{0,1,-1\}$; substitution into $A=0$ gives $w=0$, contradicting distinctness.  Hence the unique pseudoline type is not realizable over $\R$, and $B=83$ is impossible.

Combining \cref{eq:Bge80,eq:B83bound} with the four level terminals gives $q\ge61$.  The construction \cref{eq:construction} gives $q\le61$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:n13}
$f(13)=61$.
\end{theorem}
