## Elementary checks on the printed 2019 argument

**Lemma 8, page 11.** The printed assumptions are primitive integer sides and 3α+2β=π; the conclusion includes squarefreeness of g=gcd(a,c). For (a,b,c)=(4,15,16), put t=arcsin(1/8), α=2t, β=π/2−3t, γ=π/2+t. The angles are positive, sum to π and satisfy the stated relation. Their sine ratios give a/c=1/4 and b/c=15/16. The three sides have gcd 1, but gcd(a,c)=4 is not squarefree. This disproves that auxiliary assertion under its printed assumptions. It is a triangle, not a claimed tiling.

**Theorem 15, equation (20), page 66.** From N/M²=(3−s²)/(1+s)² and s=a/c, clearing denominators gives N(a+c)²=M²(3c²−a²), not N(a²+c²)=M²(3c²−a²). This is an algebraic correction, not a counterexample to the non-prime conclusion.

The useful external-review question is whether a corrected or later argument for these dependencies is available. The N71 certificate here requires neither squarefreeness of g nor the older claimed divisibility g|M.
