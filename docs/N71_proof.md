# Exact exclusion of (125,125,142)/(10,21,25)

Version: 22 September 2026. Research directed by Denis Paliy with substantial GPT/Codex assistance. This is the mathematical interface to a computer-assisted proof, not a claim of first discovery or a solution of Erdős #634.

## Theorem

A triangle with sides (125,125,142) cannot be partitioned into triangles congruent to (10,21,25). Reflections and T-junctions are allowed. The area ratio is 71.

The proof uses a root-coverage argument, a complete branching rule, sound rejection rules and a finite exact refutation. The paper argument is essential in addition to executing the checker.

## 1. Root coverage

Let α,β,γ be the tile angles opposite a=10,b=21,c=25. The cosine rule gives cos α=23/25. Thus sin(α/2)=1/5 and sin(3α/2)=71/125=cos β. The relevant angles give 3α+2β=π and γ=(π+α)/2. The target has base angles β and apex angle 3α.

The number α/π is irrational: otherwise exp(iα) would be a root of unity, and 2cos α=46/25 would be a rational algebraic integer, hence an integer, a contradiction.

At the apex B, each incident tile has its own vertex; an interior point of a tile edge would occupy a half-plane. If r,s,t tile angles occur there, rα+sβ+tγ=3α. Substitute β=(π−3α)/2 and γ=(π+α)/2. Irrationality gives s+t=0 and r=3. Exactly three α-tiles meet at B.

Each equal exterior side contains a c-edge. Otherwise its m edges of types a,b would contribute m occurrences of γ at their endpoints. Neither outer endpoint admits γ: β<γ and 3α<γ, the latter because α<β. At most one γ fits at each of the m−1 internal vertices since 2γ>π. This is impossible. A different tile cannot subdivide the interior of an exterior tile edge without entering the half-plane already occupied by that tile; the argument therefore allows T-junctions elsewhere.

Write 125=10A+21B+25C, C≥1. Modulo 5, 5 divides B. If B≥5, the side length is at least 105+25=130. Hence B=0 on both equal sides.

The sides incident to an α-angle are b,c. Thus each extreme apex tile uses c on the exterior side. This fixes the two extreme tiles. The middle α-tile has incident sides (b,c) or (c,b); reflection exchanges the two configurations.

Coordinates (x,z) below mean physical coordinates (x,√6 z). All decimals are exact:

    A=(0,0), B=(71,42), C=(142,0),
    V=(56.8,33.6), P=(66.8,33.6), U=(76,32),
    T=(75.2,33.6), W=(85.2,33.6).

It suffices to exclude extensions of (B,V,P), (B,P,U), (B,T,W). Each has squared side lengths 100,441,625 and their interiors are disjoint. The checker fixes this exact root; the certificate cannot substitute another easier root.

## 2. Complete branching

At a state all placed tiles are contained in the target with pairwise disjoint interiors. Select a strict convex sector of the unfilled region and one bounding ray.

A tile adjacent to that ray in any complete extension must have a vertex at the corner: an interior point of a tile edge would occupy angle π and cannot fit in a strict convex sector. Its edge starts along the ray.

There are 12 possible placements: three ray-edge lengths, two adjacent-edge lengths and two half-planes. Candidates leaving the target or overlapping existing tile interiors are rejected. Every genuine extension contains a surviving candidate.

The independent checker reconstructs local oriented boundary rays, including T-junction vertices, verifies that the chosen sector is empty and strictly convex, then checks that the recorded children are exactly the entire surviving candidate set. Missing children and unreachable extra states are rejected. Each added tile reduces the remaining area.

## 3. Sound rejection rules

### No candidate

All 12 placements are invalid at a verified strict convex corner. By the branching argument there is no complete extension.

### Exterior gap

An uncovered exterior segment between actual boundary vertices must consist of whole tile edges. Its length belongs to the nonnegative semigroup generated by 10,21,25. The checker verifies the exterior side, actual endpoints, absence of already covered interior and exact nonrepresentability. A future tile edge crossing such an endpoint would overlap a tile already present there in the interior half-plane.

### Narrow c/b strip

For a=uv,b=v²−u²,c=v², gcd(u,v)=1 and 0<u<v, first prove:

    j*c=A*a+B*b+C*c, 1≤j<v  implies B=0.

Modulo v write B=vℓ. If ℓ≥1, reduction of the divided equation gives A=ℓu−vt for an integer t≤ℓ−1. Therefore j=C+ℓv−ut≥v+(ℓ−1)(v−u)≥v, a contradiction. When B=0 the solutions are (A,B,C)=(vh,0,j−uh), 0≤h≤floor(j/u).

Suppose a real c-edge PR is parallel to an exterior supporting side and its tile's opposite vertex lies on that side. The strip width is the altitude to c. Every subsequent tile supported on the same line on the strip side must use a c-edge: a shorter side gives a greater altitude and leaves the target.

Suppose on the other side there is a real b-edge PQ with the same oriented start. If the ray leaves the target before distance vc, no full extension exists. Initially the endpoints are at c and b. The earlier endpoint lies inside a real opposite edge and forces another edge on the side that ended. A new edge cannot extend backward through that endpoint without overlapping its predecessor. Hence one side advances by c-steps, and the other by a,b,c-steps retaining its initial b-edge. A common endpoint would give the forbidden short equality above with B≥1. A finite tiling can neither continue indefinitely nor cross the exterior boundary. T-junctions are allowed; every continuation is forced by an actual opposite edge.

For N71 the checker verifies the two tiles, common start, c- and b-edges, opposing half-planes, exterior support and that P+5(R−P) is strictly outside the closed target. Arbitrary interior directions are not truncated.

## 4. Complete finite refutation

The original tree has 7,618 states and 7,617 transitions, with 1,798 empty-candidate leaves, 366 exterior-gap leaves and 282 strip leaves. No pending state or full tiling remains.

The generator uses integer coordinates localized at 5, rotations and separating-axis tests. The independent checker instead uses general rational coordinates, placements constructed from side lengths and altitude, polygon clipping and local-cone checks. It does not import the generator's geometry. Essential checks remain enabled under Python -O.

The original JSON SHA-256 is:

    88f3c461c1b4cd2f824499732514492f69a9548532843045ac8cb18e53eb81f3

It was replayed in the publication session and ACCEPTed for all 7,618 states. The shifted-endpoint theorem in the accompanying note gives a second 7,614-state refutation; it is not required for the original proof.

Root coverage, complete branching, sound leaves and the accepted finite tree prove the fixed-pair theorem. ∎

## Scope and priority

The conclusion is not a classification of all 71-tilings or all N. Beeson already stated a broader non-prime conclusion for this angle family in Theorem 15 of arXiv:1206.2229v3. The present work offers an exact self-contained certificate independent of the older divisibility route. Literary novelty and external review are not established.
