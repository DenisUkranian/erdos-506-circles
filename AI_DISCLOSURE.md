# AI assistance and human role

The research was directed and selected for publication by Denis Paliy. GPT/Codex systems were used extensively for mathematical exploration, proof drafting, programming, archive reconciliation, literature lookup and internal audits. This publication is not represented as an unaided human proof.

Different automated checker implementations use different coordinate representations and candidate/intersection algorithms. Their replay is a reproducible computational audit, not a substitute for human review of root coverage, geometric lemmas or exhaustiveness.

No claim is made that Denis or another human has independently checked every historical proof. No external endorsement, co-authorship or peer-review acceptance is implied by citing an author's work or discussing an error in a printed lemma.

Missing research is labelled missing. Many internal PASS logs do not establish an unrelated general conjecture. Third-party code with unclarified redistribution rights is not relabelled as original work.
